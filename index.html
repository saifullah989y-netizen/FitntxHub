Still Have Questions?

Our team is ready to guide you through every step of your transformation journey.

What is FITNTX HUB?
−
FITNTX HUB is a premium online fitness platform connecting members with expert coaches, personalized programs, progress tracking, and a global community — designed to accelerate your complete transformation.
How does the coaching work?
+
After signing up, you're matched with a certified coach based on your goals. Your coach designs your program, reviews your progress weekly, provides form feedback, and adjusts your plan as you improve.
Is FITNTX suitable for beginners?
+
Absolutely. FITNTX is built for all fitness levels. Our onboarding assesses exactly where you are and your program is designed to meet you there, progressing at your own pace.
What equipment do I need?
+
None required. We build programs for home, gym, or hybrid training. During signup, tell us what you have access to and your program will be tailored accordingly.
When will full membership access launch?
+
We're currently in pre-launch. Sign up now to secure founding member status, get early access, and receive exclusive launch pricing when we go fully live.
